# Nookscanner Support

Nookscanner turns a short walkthrough video of an apartment into a 3D tour with step-by-step
appliance guides. Guests open the tour by scanning a QR code in the apartment, so they can find
how the oven, air conditioning or Wi-Fi works without calling you.

- **Nookscanner Capture** (iPhone): record the guided walkthrough, room by room.
- **Dashboard** (web): manage properties, apartments, rooms, appliances and QR codes, and review
  3D scenes before publishing.
- **Guest tour** (web): opens from the QR code; no app or account needed.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I sign in to the app?

Use the same email and password as your Nookscanner dashboard account. The app has no separate
sign-up: create your account, organization and apartments in the dashboard first.

### Why does the app say the apartment has no rooms?

Open the apartment in the dashboard, set its layout and press "Create these rooms". The app
then shows a room-by-room plan.

### How do I get a good recording?

- Switch on all the lights and open the blinds.
- Hold the phone sideways at chest height.
- Walk slowly along the walls and capture every corner and the floor.
- Walk through each doorway into the next room, keeping the door frame in view.
- Remove personal items and documents, and don't film people.
- Follow the tips on screen (for example "Moving too fast"); you can pause at any time.

### Can I leave the app while the video uploads?

Yes. Uploads continue in the background and pick up where they stopped if the connection drops.
Open the Uploads screen to see progress.

### How do guests open the tour?

Print the apartment's QR code from the dashboard and place it in the apartment. Guests scan it
with their phone camera; the tour opens in the browser, in English or Arabic.

### How do I delete my data or my account?

Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from your account's email address and we will delete your account and
personal data within 30 days. Organization owners can ask us the same way to delete an apartment
or the whole organization; its recordings and 3D scenes are removed with it. See the
[Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for details.
